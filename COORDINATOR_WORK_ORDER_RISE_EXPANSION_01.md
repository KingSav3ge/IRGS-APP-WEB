next build cannot start, and expose the relevant expansion authority and received
terms. Offer explicit request, review and acceptance controls only for supported
Engine operations. A pending request or proposed grant must not raise the meter.
Preserve the chronological tale, latest response form, exact editable words,
actor filtering, independent drafts and uncertain-request recovery. Do not add
an App-only cap, fake expansion success or hard-code a numerical progression.
Qualify the limit, refusal, grant, retry and reload journeys through real HTTP
and the browser against exact Engine/Catalog pins.

## Delivery

Follow AGENTS.md and the standing COORDINATOR_WORK_ORDER.md. Preserve existing
source, saves, original archives, request identities, Core/K-owner/Director
boundaries and original-result recovery. Report exact commit, dependencies,
actual checks, unresolved decisions and limitations. Push only the owning
repository. Do not claim that a written instruction wakes a development chat,
implements the mechanic or qualifies a live provider or native Windows.

This supplement adds a bounded Rise requirement to the existing shared Alpha
work. It does not replace the general WorldWard development programme. Current
joined edition remains 3.4.7; the coordinator alone qualifies the next joined
publication, which must increment to 3.4.8.
