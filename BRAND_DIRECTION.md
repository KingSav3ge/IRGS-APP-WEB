# Working brand direction

The leading brand architecture is **T!DEMARCH Studios** as developer and
publisher, **WorldWard** as the player-facing platform, and **Fulcrum Engine**
as the underlying simulation technology. Use `WorldWard` in formal product
branding and `WORLDWARD` for cinematic title treatments. The preferred credit is:

> WorldWard — Developed by T!DEMARCH Studios. Powered by Fulcrum Engine.

IRGS remains the internal development initiative and repository identity. This
is a working brand direction subject to the user's stated commercial clearance;
no Catalog/Atlas naming decision or legal clearance is implied. User-facing
branding changes do not rename paths, saved data, Engine APIs, repository names,
component identities or existing historical records.
