# Continuous story and response layout

The Play view now reads from oldest available entry to newest, on one reading
surface. The next choices and editable response follow the newest entry and
share the same scroll area. This applies to Rise and other supported campaigns.
Earlier story entries are visible, with search available in Story details; Rise
no longer truncates its display to twelve receipts. Source metadata, camp/work
inspection and completed process controls remain available as expandable details.

The ordering uses actual recorded/received causal positions. It never converts
elapsed work, revision numbers or historical dates into invented event times.
Committed actor-scoped presentations appear at their recorded position; exact
matching receipt fragments are not printed twice. Additional testimony and
source-gap records remain. A process phase retains its actual active/paused/
completed status and speech retains received/offer status. Intent is not an effect.

The response form is retained while views re-render, so unsent drafts and request
recovery still work. Returning to a story follows its latest entry unless the
reader had scrolled back into earlier text. Pending intent retains its withdrawal
control; unavailable Rise choices are folded out of the reading flow until it is
resolved. Actions still require the existing explicit review and confirmation.

No Engine/Catalog source, dependency pin, save format or mechanics changed. The
attached known-view export was read and replayed in a browser only; it was not
loaded as a campaign save or committed to the repository. Test stores are synthetic.

See STORY_FLOW_UI_VALIDATION.json for executed checks and source identities.
This App update retains coordinated edition 3.4.4. The next unified publication
must use the next version and the coordinator's all-component integration gate.
